## Current Regime: "Higher for Longer," Energy-Driven Inflation Spike

The Fed is on hold despite earlier market expectations of 2026 rate cuts, because inflation re-accelerated on an energy/geopolitical shock (US-Iran tensions). This is a **stagflation-adjacent** backdrop: growth-sensitive and rate-sensitive names (e.g., SOFI, and partially CEG/POWL) are being punished for dashed rate-cut hopes, while defensive/staples and industrials with pricing power (WMT, LIN) are holding up better.
