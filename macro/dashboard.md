## FOMC Preview — 2026-06-17

**Rate**: No change expected (0.6% implied probability of hike). The meeting's real significance is the dot plot — Warsh's first SEP as Chair.

**Dot plot risk**: The March 2026 SEP showed rates on hold through 2026 (set under Powell). The June update, under Warsh, is widely expected to shift to at least neutral — and multiple previews suggest the median dot will show **at least one hike in 2026**. This would represent a hawkish surprise vs. market consensus of "on hold all year." — IndexBox, FinanceCalendar, 2026-06

**Portfolio impact**:
- **SOFI**: Most exposed. A hawkish dot plot confirms "higher for longer" and removes the rate-cut thesis underpinning a thesis-or-exit decision. Truist already cut target to $17 (right at current price). Securities investigation overhang adds noise.
- **CEG/POWL**: Secondary exposure to rate sensitivity and capex cost-of-capital. CEG is rated Accumulate; a hawkish surprise would keep near-term pressure on the stock despite strong fundamentals.
- **Less affected**: ENTG, ICHR, WMT, LIN — less rate-sensitive.
