## Core Thesis

The 2026 FIFA World Cup (USA/Mexico/Canada, expanded 48-team/104-match format) began 2026-06-11 and runs through mid-July — a once-every-four-years global consumer-attention event. Two angles: (1) **AB InBev/Budweiser (BUD)** is a long-standing FIFA sponsor with a partnership just extended through 2030 (including the Women's World Cup), giving it outsized marketing visibility during the tournament; (2) **DraftKings (DKNG)** and the broader regulated sports-betting industry are positioned for a surge in betting handle tied to World Cup matches — global wagering on this World Cup is projected well above the ~$35B wagered in 2022.

For this to work: the World Cup needs to translate into measurable revenue/volume upside for BUD (beyond brand visibility) and DKNG (handle growth flowing to revenue/EBITDA), within a fairly short window (June–mid-July 2026).

## Supporting Data

- AB InBev's FIFA partnership extended through 2030, now also covering the Women's World Cup — TIKR, 2026-05 (via Q1 2026 earnings recap). Confirms the sponsorship angle is current, not legacy.
- BUD Q1 2026 (reported 2026-05-05): revenue $15.3B (+12% YoY), adjusted EPS $0.97 (+20.8% YoY, highest-ever Q1 EPS). Stock rallied >9% intraday to ~$80.84. Moody's upgraded BUD A3 → A2. FY2026 EBITDA guidance reaffirmed +4–8%.
- DKNG Q1 2026 (reported 2026-05-07): revenue $1,646M (+17% YoY, +$237M), EPS $0.20 vs. $0.17 est. FY2026 guidance maintained: revenue $6.5–6.9B, adjusted EBITDA $700–900M. Live for mobile betting in 27 states + DC + Puerto Rico (~53% of US population).
- World Cup betting projections (Eilers & Krejcik Gaming via SportsHandle, 2026-06 — **estimates, not realized data**): US sportsbook handle for the tournament ~$2.82B base case, up to $4.3–4.4B high end. DKNG specifically estimated to capture ~$1.1B; DKNG + FanDuel projected ~70% combined US share during the tournament. Globally, $50–60B projected wagered vs. ~$35B in 2022 (CNBC, 2026-06-10, framed as potentially "the biggest gambling event in history").

## Risks

- **No hard data yet quantifying a World Cup-specific revenue lift for BUD** — the sponsorship angle is real and confirmed, but the financial impact is an inference, not a measured fact. BUD's Q1 2026 strength predates the tournament.
- **BUD's legacy US brands are declining** independent of this thesis: Bud Light volumes -12.5%, Budweiser -9.5%, Busch -12.8% YoY (Q1 2026). The World Cup tailwind, if real, is a partial offset to a structural domestic decline — not a standalone growth story.
- **DKNG World Cup estimates are pre-event projections** from industry analysts (Eilers & Krejcik), not confirmed handle data. Actual results could disappoint vs. the $1.1B DKNG-specific estimate.
- **Regulatory risk** for DKNG remains state-by-state; any adverse legislative/tax development in a major state would matter more than the World Cup tailwind.
- **Timing**: the catalyst window is short (tournament runs ~2026-06-11 to mid-July). BUD's next earnings (2026-07-30) falls just after the World Cup final — a relatively clean test of the thesis. DKNG's read-through likely won't show up until Q2/Q3 earnings (Q2 likely reported August 2026).
